# 06 — Template Reference

Complete reference for all 31 example templates included with Tidslinjal.

---

## Exercise Templates (EX-01 through EX-20)

### EX-01: Quick Reaction Force
**File:** `ex01-quick-reaction-force.json`
**Duration:** 1 day · 0800–1700 · 9 hours
**Type:** Military, co-located

Single-day rapid response exercise. A QRF unit responds to two sequential security incidents. Good introductory template showing escalation authority, SITREP discipline, and AAR structure.

**Best for:** New exercise planners; teams learning SITREP procedure; simple 1-day scenarios.

---

### EX-02: Cyber Defence Sprint
**File:** `ex02-cyber-defence-sprint.json`
**Duration:** 1 day · 0800–1700 · 9 hours
**Type:** Cyber security, co-located or virtual

Blue team defends against simulated red team. Action-reaction structure: red team injects drive blue team response tasks. Good model for adversarial exercise design.

**Best for:** IT security teams; SOC training; exercises with explicit red/blue structure.

---

### EX-03: Joint Command Post — Co-located, 48h
**File:** `ex03-joint-command-post-collocated-24h.json`
**Duration:** 2 days · 24/7 (48 hours)
**Type:** Military, co-located

Full 48-hour command post exercise with battle rhythm, shift handovers, night watch periods, and physical meeting locations. Compare with EX-05 (same schedule, virtual).

**Best for:** Command post exercises; developing battle rhythm; multi-shift 24/7 operations.

---

### EX-04: Urban Defence — Co-located, Working Hours
**File:** `ex04-urban-defence-collocated-hours.json`
**Duration:** 2 days · 0800–1700 with overnight recess
**Type:** Military, co-located

2-day exercise at a physical training facility. Day 1 planning, Day 2 execution. Demonstrates overnight recess locks and lunch breaks. Compare with EX-06 (same schedule, virtual).

**Best for:** Training facilities with defined hours; exercises where participants go home at night.

---

### EX-05: Distributed Command — Virtual, 48h
**File:** `ex05-distributed-command-virtual-24h.json`
**Duration:** 2 days · 24/7 (48 hours)
**Type:** Military/joint, virtual/distributed

Virtual counterpart to EX-03. All coordination via Teams/Mattermost. More standups, explicit channel references, connectivity verification, system maintenance windows.

**Best for:** Distributed/remote teams; participants in different cities or countries.

---

### EX-06: Staff Training — Virtual, Working Hours
**File:** `ex06-staff-training-virtual-hours.json`
**Duration:** 2 days · 0800–1700 with overnight recess
**Type:** Staff training, virtual

Virtual counterpart to EX-04. Training-focused: Day 1 delivers three training modules; Day 2 applies them. Features assessed deliverables (SITREP forms, lessons reports).

**Best for:** Staff officer training at distance; exercises with assessed outcomes.

---

### EX-07: Combined Arms Manoeuvre — 3 Days
**File:** `ex07-combined-arms-manoeuvre-3day.json`
**Duration:** 3 days · 24h D1–D2, working hours D3
**Type:** Military field exercise, co-located

3-day combined arms exercise. Find-fix-finish-exploit structure. Fire support coordination, night navigation, breach and exploitation. Most militarily realistic template.

**Best for:** Military manoeuvre exercises; combined arms integration; fire support coordination.

---

### EX-08: Crisis Management Simulation — 3 Days

**Type:** Civil crisis management, multi-agency

Civilian crisis management exercise. Multi-agency coordination (Police, Fire, Health, Transport, EOC). Public communications deadlines. Recovery phase on Day 3.

### EX-09: NATO Integration Exercise — 5 Days

### EX-10: Full Spectrum Warfare — 5 Days

## Incident Response Templates (INC-01 through INC-11)

All incident response templates use:
- `operation_mode: "incident"`
- T=0 = incident detection time
- 24/7 schedule (day_start_hour: 0, day_end_hour: 24) unless otherwise noted

## Template Selection Decision Tree

```
Is this a live incident?
├─ YES → Use INC template
│    ├─ Cyber attack? → INC-01 through INC-11
│    │    ├─ DDoS → INC-01
│    │    ├─ Ransomware → INC-04
│    │    ├─ Wiper/destructive → INC-05
│    │    ├─ APT/nation-state → INC-03
│    │    ├─ Supply chain → INC-07
│    │    ├─ Insider threat → INC-08
│    │    ├─ Phishing/BEC → INC-09
│    │    ├─ OT/ICS → INC-10
│    │    └─ Cloud breach → INC-11
│    └─ Physical disaster → INC-06
│
└─ NO (exercise/training)
     ├─ 1 day → EX-01 (military) or EX-02 (cyber) or EX-15 (special ops)
     ├─ 2 days → EX-03/05 (24/7) or EX-04/06 (working hours) or EX-11 (border) or EX-18 (air def)
     ├─ 3 days → EX-07 (military) or EX-08 (crisis) or EX-12 (maritime) or EX-17 (info ops) or EX-20 (EW)
     ├─ 4 days → EX-13 (CIMIC)
     └─ 5 days → EX-09 (NATO) or EX-10 (full spectrum) or EX-14 (logistics) or EX-19 (peacekeeping)
```
